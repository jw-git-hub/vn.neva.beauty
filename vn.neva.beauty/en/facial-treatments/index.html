Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions — Facial Treatments

What do facial treatments at Neva Beauty include?

Facial treatments at Neva Beauty in Da Nang include a deep cleansing facial, an ultrasonic cleansing facial, professional chemical peels, HydraFacial and non-invasive carboxytherapy, as well as facial masks and device-based treatments: RF (radiofrequency) therapy and ultrasound therapy. The specific program for your skin type and condition is chosen by a Neva Beauty aesthetician in Da Nang. The full list of treatments and prices is in the price list on this page.

Are facial treatments painful?

Facial treatments at Neva Beauty in Da Nang are performed with gentle, non-damaging techniques, so most of them are comfortable and do not require a lengthy recovery. The sensation depends on the treatment: a deep cleansing facial and chemical peels are felt more strongly than milder facials, while HydraFacial and masks feel gentle. The intensity is tailored individually to your skin's sensitivity.

How often should I have a facial?

At Neva Beauty in Da Nang, the number and frequency of facials are tailored individually at the consultation, depending on your skin type, its condition and the goal (cleansing, even skin tone, elasticity). Some programs give visible results after the very first treatment, and regular care helps keep the skin firm. The exact plan is drawn up by the aesthetician after a skin assessment.

How much does a facial cost in Da Nang?

The price of a facial at Neva Beauty in Da Nang depends on the treatment: cleansing facials, chemical peels, HydraFacial, non-invasive carboxytherapy and facial masks are priced separately, while add-ons (RF or ultrasound therapy, serum, alginate mask) are charged on top of the base facial. Current prices in Vietnamese dong (đ) are in the price list on this page. Related device-based rejuvenation treatments, M22 photorejuvenation and Morpheus8 RF microneedling, are priced separately.

How do I book a facial at Neva Beauty?

You can book a facial at Neva Beauty, a beauty center in Da Nang, via WhatsApp, Telegram or Instagram. Visits are by appointment only, and service is provided in Russian and English.
