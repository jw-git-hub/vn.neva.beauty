Frequently Asked Questions — Non-Surgical Body Contouring

What body contouring treatments does Neva Beauty offer?

At Neva Beauty, a beauty center in Da Nang, body contouring is done with three device-based methods: LPG massage, Endosphere therapy and ultrasonic cavitation. All of them are non-surgical and require no recovery period.

What is the difference between LPG massage, Endosphere therapy and cavitation?

LPG massage and Endosphere therapy are both forms of mechanical massage: they activate lymphatic drainage, get rid of fluid retention and cellulite, and shape the body. Ultrasonic cavitation works differently: it breaks down fat cells with ultrasound and reduces the circumference of targeted areas. At Neva Beauty in Da Nang, the right method for you is chosen at the consultation.

Are body contouring treatments painful?

No. Device-based body contouring at Neva Beauty in Da Nang is comfortable and painless, with no damage to the skin and no downtime: you can return to your usual activities right after the session.
