Non-Surgical Face Lift & Rejuvenation

HIFU Treatment

HIFU (high-intensity focused ultrasound) treatment at Neva Beauty, a beauty center in Da Nang, is a non-surgical face lift, done without surgery. Focused ultrasound acts on the deep muscle and connective-tissue layer (SMAS) and stimulates the production of your own collagen, restoring definition to the facial contours and lifting the neck and decollete area. At Neva Beauty, HIFU is a single treatment rather than a course. It is done on the face, neck and decollete, and also on body areas (abdomen, arms, thighs). Results develop gradually and are long-lasting. There is no recovery period, and the treatment is comfortable even in Da Nang's hot climate.

Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions — HIFU Treatment

Is HIFU treatment painful?

HIFU treatment at Neva Beauty in Da Nang is comfortable: during the session you feel warmth and a slight tingling at the points where the ultrasound is applied. The treatment does not damage the surface of the skin and requires no recovery period, so you can return to your usual activities right after the session. The intensity is tailored individually to your sensitivity.

How many HIFU treatments do I need?

HIFU treatment at Neva Beauty in Da Nang is a single treatment, not a course: even one session produces a noticeable lifting effect. Results develop gradually as your own collagen is produced, and they are long-lasting; the treatment is repeated as needed. The exact plan is chosen at the consultation, depending on the area and the condition of your skin.

Which areas does HIFU lift?

HIFU treatment at Neva Beauty in Da Nang lifts the facial contours, the under-jaw area, the neck, the decollete area and the delicate area around the eyes; it is also done on body areas: the abdomen, arms and thighs. Related device-based treatments for non-surgical face lifting and rejuvenation at Neva Beauty are Morpheus8 RF (radiofrequency) microneedling and M22 photorejuvenation.

How much does HIFU treatment cost in Da Nang?

The price of HIFU treatment at Neva Beauty in Da Nang depends on the area and the combo you choose: combos bring several areas together (for example, the face, under-jaw area, neck and decollete), while single areas are priced separately. Current prices in Vietnamese dong (đ) are in the price list on this page.
