Frequently Asked Questions — Laser Hair Removal

Is laser hair removal painful?

Most people find laser hair removal at Neva Beauty in Da Nang comfortable and experience it as brief warmth and a slight tingling with each pulse. Sensitivity depends on the area and your individual pain threshold, so the intensity is adjusted to your tolerance. The treatment requires no recovery period.

How many laser hair removal sessions do I need?

The number of laser hair removal sessions varies from person to person: it depends on the area, the type and color of the hair and its growth phase. Hair grows in cycles, so hair removal is done as a course with intervals between sessions, and at Neva Beauty in Da Nang the exact plan is drawn up at the consultation.

Which areas can be treated with the laser?

Laser hair removal at Neva Beauty in Da Nang is suitable for any area of the face and body for women and men: the face and upper lip, underarms, arms, legs and lower legs, back, abdomen, chest, bikini line and Brazilian. Combos of popular areas are also available — all prices are in the price list above.

How much does laser hair removal cost in Da Nang?

Laser hair removal at Neva Beauty in Da Nang costs from 150 000 đ (forehead or upper lip, women's prices). The price depends on the area, from small areas up to the full body. Current prices in Vietnamese dong (đ) for women and men are in the price list on this page. Related laser services, tattoo removal and permanent makeup removal, are priced separately.

How do I book laser hair removal?

You can book laser hair removal at Neva Beauty, a beauty center in Da Nang, via WhatsApp, Telegram or Instagram. Visits are by appointment only, and service is provided in Russian and English. A consultation with the aesthetician costs 500 000 đ; if you have a treatment, the consultation is included in the treatment price.
